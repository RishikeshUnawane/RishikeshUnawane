## Hi, I'm Rishikesh 👋

I'm a software engineer at Microsoft, working on the Microsoft Teams iOS app, which tens of millions of people open every day to talk to their colleagues. I mostly work on media and search, the parts of the app people only notice when they break.

At that scale, the interesting problems are rarely the obvious ones. A photo upload that works fine on office Wi-Fi has to survive a flaky mobile connection on the other side of the world. A video has to be usable by someone who can't hear the audio. Search has to return the right person, message or file in a fraction of a second, across a huge amount of data. Every iOS release can quietly break something that worked yesterday. Much of my job is finding and fixing these edge cases before millions of users run into them, while still shipping new features from idea to launch.

Lately I've been spending my time on agentic AI, building agents and workflows that do real work, not just demos. I'm especially interested in the gap between a model that looks smart and a system you can trust in production.

Before Microsoft, I studied Computer Engineering at Vishwakarma Institute of Technology in Pune. There I led a small team that replaced a messy Google Forms process with a proper reporting platform for ~1,200 students, cutting a month-long cycle down to a week. It was my first real lesson in how much good software can change someone's workflow.
